Märchenfuchs tells children bedtime stories that exist only once: with their own name and their favorite topics, matched to their age. Parents set up once what their child loves, and the app turns that into a new story every evening.

It is more than a text generator. The stories follow age-appropriate story patterns, and parents stay in control of topics and length. To keep quality from coming down to luck, a separate review step scores every story against a fixed rubric, with a set of reference cases as the benchmark.

The app is built and running, the payment model is in place, and new features go to a separate development instance before they go live. Right now we are looking for our first beta families before we open up.

The three of us are building Märchenfuchs as Racep Labs. I am responsible for the technical side: the Flutter app, the Supabase backend and the story pipeline behind it.
