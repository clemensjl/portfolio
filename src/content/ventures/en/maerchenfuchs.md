Märchenfuchs tells children bedtime stories that exist only once: with their own name, their favorite topics, matched to their age. Parents set up once what their child loves; the app turns that into a new story every evening.

There is more behind it than a text generator: stories follow age-appropriate narrative patterns, and parents stay in control of topics and length. So quality does not come down to luck, a dedicated evaluation stage scores every story against a fixed rubric, with a set of reference cases as the benchmark.

The app is built and running, the payment model is in place, and a separate development instance takes new features before they go live. Right now we are looking for the first beta families before opening up.

We are building Märchenfuchs as a team of three under Racep Labs. I own the technical side: the Flutter app, the Supabase backend and the story pipeline behind it.
