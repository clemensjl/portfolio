SpecMatch lines up current devices and makes their specs directly comparable, so you do not have to dig through a dozen manufacturer pages. Each row of the spec sheet shows which device wins, with the overall score at the bottom.

The catalog has 196 products in seven categories (phones, laptops, tablets, headphones, smartwatches, monitors and smart home), from current devices like the iPhone 17, Galaxy S26 and M5 MacBook to older models for upgrade comparisons.

The interface is deliberately designed like a technical data sheet, dark mode included. It also works without a mouse: a skip link, focus traps in dialogs, ARIA labels and support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
