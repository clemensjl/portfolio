SpecMatch puts current devices next to each other and makes their specs directly comparable, instead of collecting them from a dozen manufacturer pages. Every row of the spec sheet marks which device wins, with the overall tally underneath.

The catalog holds 196 products across seven categories — phones, laptops, tablets, headphones, smartwatches, monitors and smart home — from current devices like the iPhone 17, Galaxy S26 and the M5 MacBook down to older models for upgrade comparisons.

The interface is deliberately built like a technical data sheet, dark mode included. It works without a mouse too: skip link, focus trap in dialogs, ARIA labels and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
